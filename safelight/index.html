    Breathe

    Why this works

    It takes what it's given. The subconscious doesn't weigh whether a thought is good for you. It accepts what's repeated and acts on it.

    Feeling is the key. One sentence said with real feeling does more than a thousand said flatly. Straining is what stops it taking.

    Never fight a thought. Arguing with a fear feeds it your attention. Don't wrestle it — replace it.

    Sleep is the doorway. The last five minutes before sleep are worth more than an hour at noon. That's what the night session is for.

    You don't need the how. Decide the outcome and drop the route. Instructing yourself on the method is how you talk yourself out of it.
