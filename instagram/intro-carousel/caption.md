## Posting notes

- **Upload order:** slide1 → slide2 → slide3 → slide4 → slide5 → slide6
- **Ratio:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Tallest ratio Instagram allows — don't let
  the uploader crop to square.
- **Cover:** slide1. The only one that has to stop a scroll.
- **Pin it** to the top of your profile.
- **Six is the practical ceiling.** Instagram allows 20, but swipe-through
  falls off hard after about six.
- **Alt text** (Accessibility → Write alt text), per slide:
  1. "Sanika smiling under a cherry blossom tree in a pink top. Handwritten: hi, I'm Sanika — born '01, scorpio, still becoming."
  2. "A strawberry matcha latte held over a sidewalk. Handwritten: this is where I keep the things I don't want to forget."
  3. "Sanika eating a slice of pizza in a car. Handwritten: most of my favorite meals happened somewhere like this."
  4. "A bakery case full of croissants and brioche doughnuts. Handwritten: I make most things from scratch — I come here for the ones I can't yet."
  5. "Sanika at a hot pot restaurant with a divided broth pot on the table. Handwritten: the table is the point — the food is just how we stay longer."
  6. "Sanika resting her chin on her hand beside a bouquet of pink peonies and roses. Handwritten: I'm looking for the people who notice the same small things. Stay awhile."
