## Two lines worth sharpening

- **Slide 5 — "even the bread."** You named a specific bread and the audio
  didn't come through clearly. Whatever it was, put it in: *"even the
  ciabatta, first attempt"* reads far better than the generic version.
  One line in `build/build.py`.
- **Slide 1 — the sticky-note wall.** Slide 4 says "yes, the sticky-note
  wall is real," which is fun, but if those numbers are a countdown or a
  tracker of something, saying what it is would make slide 1 the strongest
  opener in the set.

## The one thing I couldn't build

The reference traces a **white cutout outline around the subject** on some
slides. That needs the figure separated from its background, which isn't
reliable in code. It's about two minutes a slide by hand: in Canva,
Procreate or Picsart, select the subject, add an 8–12px white stroke,
nudge it slightly. Slides 1, 4 and 12 are the ones worth doing.

## Posting notes

- **Upload order:** slide01 → slide12, in numbered order
- **Ratio:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Tallest ratio Instagram allows — don't let
  the uploader crop to square.
- **Cover:** slide01. The only one that has to stop a scroll.
- **Twelve is long.** Instagram allows 20, but most people stop swiping
  around six. Slides 1–6 carry the whole introduction; for a shorter cut,
  drop 7–11 and keep 12 as the closer.
- **Pin it** to the top of your profile.
