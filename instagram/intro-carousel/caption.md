## The one thing I couldn't build

The inspo traces a **hand-drawn white outline around the subject** on
several slides — that cutout-sticker effect. That needs the subject cut
out from the background, which I can't do reliably in code. It's about
two minutes per slide by hand: in Canva, Procreate or Picsart, select the
subject, add a white stroke of 8–12px, and nudge it slightly. Worth doing
on slides 1, 5 and 11 if you want it — those are the ones where a figure
sits against a contrasting background.

## Posting notes

- **Upload order:** slide01 → slide11, in numbered order
- **Ratio:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Tallest ratio Instagram allows — don't let
  the uploader crop to square.
- **Cover:** slide01. The only one that has to stop a scroll.
- **Eleven is long.** Instagram allows 20, but most people stop swiping
  around six. Slides 1–6 carry the whole introduction on their own, so
  if you want a shorter version, cut 7–10 and keep 11 as the closer.
- **Pin it** to the top of your profile.
- **Alt text** (Accessibility → Write alt text), per slide:
   1. "Sanika under a cherry blossom tree. Handwritten: hello! I'm Sanika — born 2001, Scorpio, still figuring it out."
