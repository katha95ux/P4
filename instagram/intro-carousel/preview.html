Posting notes

Small things that make a measurable difference.

Ratio
Keep it at 4:5. It’s the tallest ratio Instagram allows, so it takes up the most feed space — don’t let the uploader crop to square.
Cover
Slide 1. It’s the only one that has to stop a scroll.
Pin it
Pin the post to the top of your profile so it stays the first thing new visitors see.
Six is the practical ceiling
Instagram allows 20, but swipe-through falls off hard after about six — slides 5 and 6 are already doing well to be seen at all.
Freed up for later
The homemade pizza, the fitting-room selfie, the windy-hair portrait, the brown bomber shot and the dusk sunset are all out of the carousel and all strong enough to headline their own post.

Built as four self-contained HTML slides — handwriting is Caveat, the swashes, sparkles, hearts and the scorpio glyph are inline SVG, and every photo carries the same airy faded-film grade — matte lifted blacks, brightened midtones, a breath of cream haze — tuned per photo so a dim hot-pot restaurant and a backlit portrait under blossoms land in the same world. On slides 1–3 the frame is extended above the photo and the scene continued upward, which is where the text gets its clean room. Source and build scripts are in instagram/intro-carousel/.
