Posting notes

Small things that make a measurable difference.

Ratio
Keep it at 4:5. It’s the tallest ratio Instagram allows, so it takes up the most feed space — don’t let the uploader crop to square.
Cover
Slide 1. It’s the only one that has to stop a scroll.
Pin it
Pin the post to the top of your profile so it stays the first thing new visitors see.
Slide 4 runs light on purpose
The first three slides are warm and moody; the last opens up into white with brown ink, so the swipe ends on an exhale. Say the word and it becomes a dark slide with cream text like the others.
Freed up for later
The brown bomber portrait and the dusk sunset shot are both strong enough to headline their own post — the bomber one for an outfit post, the sunset for a “little moments lately” photo dump.

Built as four self-contained HTML slides — handwriting is Caveat, the swashes, sparkles, hearts and the scorpio glyph are inline SVG, and every photo carries the same warm film grade (lifted blacks, warm highlights, fine grain) so the set reads as one. On slides 1 and 2 the frame is extended above the photo and the scene continued upward, which is where the text gets its clean room; slide 4 flips to brown ink and ochre on a light scrim, because cream would vanish on that white wall. Source and build scripts are in instagram/intro-carousel/.
