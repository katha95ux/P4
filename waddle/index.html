Why this actually works

It takes what it's given. Your subconscious doesn't judge whether a thought is good for you. It accepts what's repeated and gets to work.

Feeling is the key. One sentence said with real feeling beats a thousand said flatly. Straining is what stops it landing.

Never argue with a thought. Fighting a worry feeds it your attention. Don't wrestle it — swap it.

Sleep is the doorway. The last five minutes before you drop off are worth more than an hour at noon. That's what the sleepy version is for.

You don't need the how. Pick the outcome, drop the route. Explaining the method to yourself is how you talk yourself out of it.
