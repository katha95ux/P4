### The diagnosis in one line
**Reach is fine for a brand-new account. Conversion is the problem.** ~9,000 people saw the content and ~31 followed. That is a ~0.3% follow rate. A focused account converts at 1–3%. With the same views you should already be at 100–250 followers.

2. **Bio doesn't sell a reason to follow.** "Main character in my own little world / oversharing through reels / vibes & questionable decisions" is cute, but it tells a new visitor nothing about *what* you post or *who* it's for.
3. **Recent posts are drifting into the weakest lane.** Your newest three (intro, "loves reading", book stack) are all at 250–450. Book content is your lowest-performing pillar. Food and matcha are your highest.
4. **Format inconsistencies leak views.** The cutting-board reel has black bars (not full 9:16). The carousel got 89 views — carousels get almost no non-follower distribution on a new account. Several covers have no title text, so the grid doesn't read as a "channel."

## 3b. Make them watch to the end, then follow

Instagram pushes a reel to more people when viewers (a) watch most of it, (b) rewatch it, and (c) share or save it. Followers come when the reel ends with a reason to come back. Build every reel like this:

| Second | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | Face + the *result* on screen ("this matcha changed my mornings"), or the question ("does this actually work?") | Stops the scroll |
| 1.5–3 | Open loop: "…but wait for the end" / "the third one is the winner" | Makes leaving feel like missing out |
| Middle | Fast, clean steps. New visual every 2–3 s. Text that *adds* info, not repeats the voice | Keeps watch-time high |
| Last 2 s | The payoff (the sip, the reveal, the finished plate) + one line: "ep 3 Thursday" / "save this" | Rewatch + follow |

**Retention formats that reliably hold to the end**
1. **Ranked lists that count down** — "3 matchas ranked, #1 shocked me." Nobody leaves before #1.
2. **Transformations** — before → after in one shot (a plain latte → the pretty one, messy desk → 5 PM reset).
3. **"Does this work?" experiments** — your lawn reel, your AI recipes. Payoff is at the end by definition.
4. **Loops** — 7–9 second reels where the last frame matches the first. Instagram counts each replay as watch time, and short loops are your fastest route to 10K+ views.
5. **Progress series** — "day 1 / day 7 / day 30" (sticky-note wall). People follow *to see the update.*
6. **Comment-bait endings** — "which one should I try next?" Comments push reach; your replies push it again.

**Follower-conversion mechanics (the profile has to close the deal)**
- **Pin three reels** that show the three pillars: best matcha reel, sticky-note wall, one cozy-food reel. Replace the intro pin — visitors want proof, not a hello.
- **Every series gets a name and a number** in the title ("Matcha Maxing · Ep 4"). Numbered episodes are the single biggest follow driver on small accounts.
- **Bio promise + posting days** so a visitor knows exactly what they're subscribing to.
- **Highlights** that mirror the pillars, with clean covers.
- **Reply to every comment as a question** ("have you tried it iced?"). Replies pull people back to the reel, and people who comment twice usually follow.
- **Stories every day** — polls, "which flavor next?", a peek behind the reel. Story viewers become followers because they've now seen you as a person, not just one reel.

## 4. Format rules (non-negotiable for 30 days)

- **9:16 only.** No black bars, no landscape. Re-shoot the cutting-board style clips vertical.
- **Face + title text in the first frame.** Your two best reels both do this. Make it every reel.
- **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds,** spoken *and* on screen. Formulas that fit your voice: "I can't believe this worked", "nobody told me…", "POV: you decided to romanticize your life", "I asked AI to…".
- **Length:** 7–15 s for reach reels (food, matcha, hacks); 30–45 s for storytelling reels. Never over 60 s for now.
- **Cover image with a title** on every reel so the grid reads like a channel, not a camera roll.
- **Trending audio at low volume** under your voice. Check the Reels audio "trending ↗" arrow.
- **Captions written for search:** first line = the hook, then 2–3 sentences with keywords (matcha, cozy, aesthetic, romanticize your life, Nashville / Knoxville if the reel is local). Instagram indexes captions now — treat them like SEO.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 max, specific. e.g. `#matchalover #cozyaesthetic #romanticizeyourlife #thatgirl #mainchara`. Skip generic ones like #reels #viral.
- **End with a soft CTA:** "part 2 on Thursday", "save this for your next grocery run", "follow for the next episode."
- **Carousels:** only as a follow-up to a reel that already did well, never as fresh content on a new account.

## 5. When to post (Tennessee)

Tennessee is split across two time zones: Nashville / Memphis are **Central**, Knoxville / Chattanooga are **Eastern**. Times below are your **local** time.

Aesthetic / cozy content is scrolled hardest in the evening and on weekend mornings. Two windows to test:

| Slot | Local time | Who's awake |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning** | 7:00–8:30 AM | Commute scroll; also strong Sat/Sun 9–11 AM for cozy content |
| **Evening** (primary) | 7:00–9:00 PM | Prime scroll window for lifestyle reels |
| Lunch (test only) | 12:00–1:00 PM | US lunch break |

**Best days:** Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday as your core 3. Add Sunday evening as a 4th once you're consistent (lifestyle/cozy content over-performs on Sundays).

**Cadence:** 3–4 reels/week, same slots every week for 30 days. Consistency matters more than the exact minute. Post Stories daily (even one) so the profile looks alive when a reel viewer taps through.

After ~100 followers, open **Professional Dashboard → Insights → Total followers → Most active times** and move your slots to match your real audience. Until then, test the two windows above and keep whichever wins after 6 posts each.

## 6. The daily 20 minutes that converts views into follows

1. **Reply to every comment within the first hour** of posting. Ask a question back — replies count as engagement and keep the reel in distribution.
2. **Spend 10 minutes commenting** (real sentences, not emojis) on 5–10 reels from creators in your niche with 5K–100K followers. Their audience is your audience.
3. **Reply to Story replies and DMs** the same day. Those people are 5× more likely to follow.
4. **Once a week, do a Collab post** with another small creator (co-author toggle). Both audiences see it.
5. **Don't delete under-performers.** Reels can pick up days later. Only remove the black-bar one if you re-shoot it.

## 7. What to measure (ignore likes)

Open each reel's Insights and track:

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Follows per reel | ≥ 1% of views (10 follows per 1,000 views) | The metric you actually care about |
| Watch time / "3-second holds" | > 70% of viewers past 3 s | Hook quality |
| Shares + Saves | Saves on recipes, shares on story reels | Signals Instagram uses to push non-follower reach |
| Non-follower % of views | Stays high (>90% now, fine) | You are being distributed; keep it up |

Review every Sunday. Double down on the top 2 reels of the week; kill any format that lands under 300 twice in a row.

## 8b. The money roadmap (PR, collabs, paid deals)

Honest framing first: at 31 followers, brands are not looking yet. But the accounts that get PR at 2K and paid deals at 10K are the ones that *built for it from day 1*. Here is how, in the order it actually happens.

### Stage 1 — 0 → 1,000 followers (now, roughly months 1–3)
**Goal:** prove a niche, a look, and an audience that responds.
- **Build "brand-ready" formats now** so your grid is a portfolio: product-led reels (a matcha powder, a frother, a candle, a bag) where the product is the hero and beautifully shot. Brands scroll your grid and ask "can I see my product in this?"
- **Start affiliate links today** — no follower minimum: Amazon Associates (matcha kits, frothers, glassware), plus apply to **LTK** and **ShopMy** once you have ~20 posts. Put one link-in-bio page (Beacons / Linktree) with "shop my matcha setup."
- **Do UGC (user-generated content) for brands** — this is the real early money. UGC means you make the video, the brand posts it; your follower count doesn't matter, your filming does. List yourself on Collabstr, Join Brands, Brands Meet Creators, and Aspire. Make 3 spec ads (a matcha brand, a skincare product, a kitchen tool) using your existing style. Typical nano UGC rates: $100–$300 per video.
- **Tag brands in every product reel** (matcha powder, café, bag, skincare). Brands search their tags. This is how the first gifted collab happens.
- **Local first:** DM 5 cafés / matcha bars / bakeries in your city with a 2-line pitch and a link to your matcha reel. Offer a free "rating" reel in exchange for the drinks. Local businesses say yes at 100 followers; national brands don't.

### Stage 2 — 1K → 5K followers
**Goal:** first PR packages and gifted collabs.
- Make a **one-page media kit**: who you are, the vibe, 3 best reels with views, average views per reel, audience age/location (from Insights), and rates. Update it monthly.
- **Pitch 10 brands a week** via DM + email (their PR email is usually in the bio). Template in §8c.
- Sign up for **Influenster**, **Octoly**, and brand ambassador programs of matcha / skincare brands you already use — these send free product to small creators for reviews.
- Start **Instagram Subscriptions / Gifts** as soon as they unlock for your account (eligibility varies by region; check Professional Dashboard → Monetization).
- Turn on **Collab posts** with 2–3 creators your size every month. Shared audiences are the fastest follower growth at this stage.

### Stage 3 — 5K → 10K+ followers
**Goal:** paid deals.
- With 5K+ engaged followers and consistent 5–10K views/reel, you are a **nano/micro influencer**. Typical paid rates: **$100–$500 per reel at 5–10K**, **$500–$1,500 at 10–50K**, more with usage rights or exclusivity.
- Never accept "exposure only" from a national brand at this stage — gifted is fine for products you'd buy anyway; anything with a deliverable list is paid.
- Package deals: 1 reel + 3 stories + usage rights. Always charge extra for usage rights and whitelisting.

### What brands actually check (build these numbers)
| Metric | Where | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Average views per reel (last 10) | Insights | > follower count |
| Engagement rate (likes+comments+saves+shares ÷ reach) | Insights | 3–6% |
| Saves and shares on product reels | Reel insights | Higher than likes |
| Consistency | Your grid | 3–4 reels/week, same look |
| Audience location | Insights → followers | Matters for US brands: mostly US |
