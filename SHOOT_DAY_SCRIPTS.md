# Shoot Day Pack — 9 reels in one day, 3 weeks of posts

Post schedule: **Tue · Thu · Sat, 7–9 PM local.** 9 reels = 3 weeks.
Mix: 3 voiceover · 3 talking-head ("yapping") · 3 music-only.

---

## Part 1 — The shoot day

### Prep the night before
- Charge phone + power bank. Clear 20 GB of storage.
- Wipe the kitchen counter and one corner of the bedroom/living room. These are your two sets.
- Lay out **3 outfits** (so 9 reels don't look like one day): A = the white crochet top, B = the light-blue long sleeve, C = a black top. Two hairstyles: down, then half-up.
- Props on a tray: matcha powder(s), frother, your clear cherry glass, milk/oat milk, ice, strawberry or vanilla syrup, a candle, your Coach bag + everything that goes in it, a book, skincare, a notebook + pen, the sticky-note wall as-is, groceries for the dinner reel.
- Print or write the shot list below on paper so you're not checking your phone.

### Gear
- Phone on a **tripod at chest height** for talking heads, **overhead or 45° for food**.
- Shoot **4K, 30fps, 9:16, exposure locked** (tap and hold on the subject).
- Talking heads: phone within 1 m of your mouth, or use wired earbuds as a mic. Voiceovers are recorded **separately at night** in a closet with Voice Memos.
- Window light only. Face the window. No overhead lights on.

### Timeline
| Time | Set | Outfit | Shoot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:00–10:30 | Kitchen, window light | B | Reel 1 (matcha ranking) + Reel 9 (matcha loop) |
| 10:30–11:30 | Kitchen | B | Reel 4 (AI dinner — prep + cook shots; plating later if needed) |
| 11:30–12:30 | Bedroom / sticky wall | A | Reel 2 (talking head: rules) + Reel 5 (wall update) |
| 12:30–1:00 | Break | — | Eat the dinner reel food for lunch |
| 1:00–2:00 | Bedroom corner, blanket | A, hair half-up | Reel 6 (what's in my bag — hands only) |
| 2:00–3:00 | Anywhere | C | Reel 7 (talking head: unpopular opinions) + Reel 3 talking parts |
| 4:30–6:00 | Golden hour, bedroom | C | Reel 8 (5 PM reset) + B-roll for Reel 3 (romanticizing a Tuesday) |
| Night | Closet | — | Record all voiceovers, 3 takes each |

**Shoot every clip twice.** Every shot lasts 5+ seconds even if you'll use 2. Get a face-in-frame first shot for every reel.

### Reel 1 — "I tested 3 matchas so you don't have to" (VO · 20–25 s)
**Series:** Matcha Maxing · Ep 2 · **Pillar:** matcha
**Why:** Countdown holds viewers to #1; product tags attract brands.
Use 3 different powders if you have them. If you only have one, make it "3 ways I make matcha, ranked": iced strawberry, hot vanilla, plain iced.

**Cover text:** `Matcha Maxing · Ep 2 — 3 ranked`
**Hook (0–2 s):** you at the counter, three glasses lined up, you look at camera.
On-screen: *"3 matchas ranked. #1 surprised me."*

**Shot list**
| # | Shot | Sec | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Face + 3 glasses | 2 | 3 matchas ranked · #1 surprised me |
| 2 | Overhead: whisk/froth matcha #3 | 3 | #3 — [name/flavor] |
| 3 | Pour over ice, close-up | 2 | grainy. clumpy. pretty though |
| 4 | Sip, small reaction | 2 | 6/10 |
| 5 | Froth #2 | 3 | #2 — [name/flavor] |
| 6 | Pour + sip | 3 | smooth, sweet, would repeat · 8/10 |
| 7 | #1: slow pour, best light | 4 | #1 — [name/flavor] |
| 8 | Sip, eyes close, tiny nod | 3 | 10/10. this is the one. |

Number two: smooth, a little sweet, I'd buy it again. Eight. And number one… I didn't expect this one to win. It's creamy, it's not bitter, it's the one I keep reaching for. Ten. **Caption:** 3 matchas, one clear winner 🍵 Ep 2 of Matcha Maxing — which one should I test next? #matchalover #matchalatte #cozyaesthetic #romanticizeyourlife #matchamaxing
**Audio:** Trending soft lo-fi at 15% under VO. **Tag** every powder brand.

### Reel 2 — "3 rules I live by as a main character" (TH · 25–30 s)
**Pillar:** rituals **Why:** Yapping reels build the "her" that people follow. Listicle = retention.

**Cover text:** `3 rules I live by`
**Setup:** sit at the sticky-note wall or bed, chest-up, window on your face. Hands visible.

**Script (say it, don't read it)**
> Three rules I live by, because I decided to be the main character of my own little world.
> One. Nothing boring gets to be boring. If I have to do laundry, I light the candle and make the matcha first. Same laundry, different movie.
> Two. I don't wait for a reason to wear the good outfit. Tuesday is the reason.
> Three. I write it on the wall. *(point at sticky notes)* If it's not on the wall, it's a wish. If it's on the wall, it's a plan.
> That's it. Which one are you stealing?

**On-screen text:** big numerals "1 · 2 · 3" appearing as you say them, plus 3-word summaries: *make it a movie · tuesday is the reason · put it on the wall*.
**Edit:** cut every pause. Zoom-punch 10% on each number.
**Caption:** the 3 rules that made my regular life feel like a good one 🎀 which one are you taking? #mainchara #romanticizeyourlife #thatgirl #cozyaesthetic #20somethings
**Audio:** none or very quiet piano. Captions ON.

## Part 4 — Editing rules (CapCut or the Instagram editor)

- 9:16, no borders. Cover: same font, title in the top third, every time.
- Captions on for every talking/VO reel. Auto-captions, then fix typos.
- Cut every breath and pause. If a clip feels long, it is.
- Text appears **with** the cut, never late.
- VO reels: music at 15–40%. Music reels: full volume, cut on the beat.
- Export at highest quality. Upload from the phone, not from desktop.
- Before posting, watch it once with the sound off. If it doesn't make sense silently, add text.
