## Reel 1 — "One week of outfits in 15 seconds" (MUS · 12–15 s)
**Why:** transition reels get rewatched. Shows range; brands see a fashion-capable creator.
**Setup:** tripod, full-body, same spot, same framing every time. Mark your feet with tape.
**Shot:** for each of 7 outfits, do the **same action** — jump, or cover the lens with your hand, or spin. Cut on the action so it looks like one motion.
**On-screen text per outfit:** `mon · coffee run` / `tue · errands but make it cute` / `wed · pretend meeting` / `thu · dinner for one` / `fri · going out (staying in)` / `sat · farmers market` / `sun · reset`
**Cover:** `7 days · 7 outfits`
**Caption:** a week in my closet 🎀 which day would you wear? every piece tagged. #ootdweek #outfitideas #fallfashion #cozyaesthetic #transitionreel
**Audio:** trending transition sound, cut hard on the beat. Tag every brand.

## Reel 2 — "Things in my apartment that make it look expensive (none of them were)" (VO · 20–25 s)
**Why:** home content saves like crazy; every item is a tag = brand discovery.
**Shot list:** slow pan of the room (2 s) → then one close-up per item, 2–3 s each, hand entering frame to touch/adjust it: the candle, a lamp, the checkerboard board, a glass vase with grocery-store flowers, a stack of books as a riser, a tray on the counter, the throw folded on the bed, a framed print.
**Voiceover**
> Things in my apartment that make it look expensive, and none of them were.
> A lamp instead of the big light. That's it, that's the whole trick.
> A tray. Everything looks intentional on a tray.
> Grocery flowers in a glass I already had.
> Books stacked as a riser, because nobody stacks books flat.
> One good candle, always visible, sometimes lit.
> A throw folded, not thrown.
> Total: less than one dinner out. Prices in the caption.
**Text:** item name + price on each shot. Final: *"less than one dinner out."*
**Cover:** `looks expensive · wasn't`
**Caption:** every item and what it cost 👇 [list] #apartmentdecor #cozyhome #homeonabudget #aesthetichome #romanticizeyourlife

## Reel 3 — "Rating my own outfits like a stranger" (TH · 25–30 s)
**Why:** self-roast comedy = comments + rewatches. Uses 5 outfits with zero transitions needed.
**Setup:** talking head in outfit 1, then hard cut to full-body in each outfit while your VO judges it.
**Script**
> I'm going to rate my own outfits like I don't know her.
> *(outfit 1)* Okay, she's giving "has a Pinterest board and it shows." Seven.
> *(outfit 2)* This is a woman who will cancel plans and look great doing it. Eight.
> *(outfit 3)* Ma'am, that's a pajama with confidence. Six. Respectfully.
> *(outfit 4)* Oh, she has money. She does not. Nine.
> *(outfit 5)* The bag is doing all the work and everyone knows it. Eight, bag gets a ten.
> Rate them in the comments. Be nicer than me.
**Text:** rating number pops in big on each outfit.
**Cover:** `rating my outfits like a stranger`
**Caption:** judging myself so you don't have to 💅 rate them, be honest #outfitcheck #rateme #ootd #fallfashion #mainchara

## Reel 4 — "Get ready with me for absolutely nothing" (TH / GRWM · 40–60 s)
**Why:** GRWM is the #1 yapping format. Longer reels build the "I know her" feeling that converts followers.
**Setup:** phone propped at the vanity/mirror, you doing your real skincare + makeup + hair. Talk the whole time. Cut the dead air later.
**Story to tell (pick one, tell it like you're on FaceTime):**
- the most questionable decision you made this week
- the thing you bought that you're now defending
- a tiny drama (the delivery guy, the neighbor's dog, the group chat)
**Structure**
> *(0–3 s, looking at camera)* Get ready with me for nothing, I have zero plans, and I need to tell you about [thing].
> *(skincare)* So on Tuesday…
> *(makeup)* …and I'm not saying I was wrong, but…
> *(hair)* …anyway. That's why I'm not allowed to [thing] anymore.
> *(final look, spin)* And I look great for nobody. Which is the point.
**Text:** product names small, bottom-left as you use them. Captions on.
**Cover:** `grwm for nothing`
**Caption:** grwm for absolutely no reason + the story I've been holding in 🎀 products tagged #grwm #storytime #getreadywithme #cozyaesthetic #yapsession

## Reel 5 — "My apartment at 7 AM vs 7 PM" (MUS · 12–15 s)
**Why:** same shots, two lights = a satisfying visual loop; shows the whole home without a "tour."
**Shoot the exact same 6 shots twice**, tripod positions marked with tape: bed → kitchen counter → window corner → desk → bathroom shelf → the front door. Morning: curtains open, bright. Evening: lamps + candle only.
**Edit:** either split-screen (top 7 AM, bottom 7 PM) or alternate cuts on the beat.
**Text:** `7 am` / `7 pm` in corners. Final: *"same apartment. different movie."*
**Cover:** `7 am vs 7 pm`
**Caption:** same rooms, two moods 🌤️🕯️ which one are you? #apartmenttour #cozyhome #7amvs7pm #aesthetichome #slowliving

## Reel 6 — "I rearranged one corner and now I don't want to leave" (VO · 20–25 s)
**Why:** transformation format. Payoff at the end = full watch.
**Shot list:** "before" — the corner as-is, honest (3 s) → hands moving things, fast cuts, 6–8 clips (10 s) → slow reveal pan of the finished corner (4 s) → you sitting in it with a drink (that's your one matcha cameo) (3 s).
**Voiceover**
> This corner has been a "someday" for three months. Today is someday.
> Lamp moves. Chair turns to face the window, not the wall. Books go vertical. Blanket goes here. Candle goes there.
> Twenty minutes. Zero dollars.
> And now I live here. I'm not leaving. Tell the mail to come to the corner.
**Text:** *before* → *20 min later* → *zero dollars*.
**Cover:** `one corner · 20 minutes`
**Caption:** the "someday" corner finally got its day 🪴 20 min, $0 #cozycorner #apartmentmakeover #beforeandafter #homedecor #romanticizeyourlife

## Reel 7 — "Me vs me: going out edition" (TH skit · 20–25 s)
**Why:** POV skits with you playing 2–3 characters = rewatch + share. Outfit changes *are* the joke.
**Characters:** **Going-Out Me** (the red top, hair done) · **Staying-In Me** (hoodie, claw clip) · **The Judge** (blazer or the crochet top, glasses).
**Shoot each character separately on the same tripod spot, left / right / center frame, then cut together.**
**Script**
> **Going-Out Me:** We said we'd go. We have the outfit. Look at the outfit.
> **Staying-In Me:** The outfit can be worn on the couch. I checked.
> **Going-Out Me:** It's Friday.
> **Staying-In Me:** There's a candle. There's a book. There's a *blanket* situation.
> **The Judge:** *(adjusts glasses)* The outfit will be worn. On the couch. Case closed.
> **Going-Out Me + Staying-In Me together:** …we love a compromise.
**Text:** character names as name-tags at the bottom.
**Cover:** `me vs me · friday`
**Caption:** the friday night negotiation 🧑‍⚖️ who wins in your house? #povskit #mevsme #introvertlife #fridaynight #mainchara

## Reel 8 — "Everything I bought this month, ranked by regret" (TH + close-ups · 30 s)
**Why:** haul + honesty = trust. Brands watch this format closely. Tag everything.
**Setup:** products laid on the bed. Talking head at the start and end; VO over close-ups for the middle. Rank from most regret to least.
**Script**
> Everything I bought this month, ranked by regret. Starting with the one I'm embarrassed about.
> *(item)* Regret: high. I've used it once. It's pretty though.
> *(item)* Regret: medium. It works, I just didn't need a fourth one.
> *(item)* Regret: low. Used daily. Would rebuy.
> *(item)* Regret: zero. Personality-changing. If you buy one thing from this reel, it's this.
> Total spent in the caption. Judge me gently.
**Text:** `regret: high / medium / low / zero` stamped on each item.
**Cover:** `bought this month · ranked by regret`
**Caption:** total in the comments 🙈 what should I buy next month? #monthlyhaul #honestreview #noregrets #cozyaesthetic #thatgirl

## Reel 9 — "Closet cleanout: keep, sell, cry" (TH · 30–40 s)
**Why:** interactive — every piece is a mini decision, comments pile up. Perfect use of a big closet.
**Setup:** sit on the closet floor with a pile. Three spots on the floor labeled with sticky notes: KEEP · SELL · CRY. Hold up each piece, decide fast.
**Script (improvise around this)**
> Closet cleanout. Three piles: keep, sell, and cry — for the ones I love but never wear.
> *(piece)* Keep. Obviously. She's the whole personality.
> *(piece)* Sell. I bought this for a version of me that goes hiking.
> *(piece)* …Cry. *(hug it, put it down)* She's beautiful and I've worn her once.
> *(piece)* Sell. Comment if you want it, I'm serious.
> *(piece)* Keep. This one gets worn on days I need to win.
> Final count in the caption. Should I actually sell the sell pile?
**Text:** the pile name stamps on screen each time. Final frame: three piles from above with counts.
**Cover:** `keep · sell · cry`
**Caption:** keep [n] · sell [n] · cry [n] 😭 should I list the sell pile? #closetcleanout #declutter #wardrobe #fallcloset #mainchara
